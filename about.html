What Loona Finance Tracker actually does

Loona Finance Tracker is a complete personal money manager that runs entirely in your web browser. It was built to replace the notebook, the spreadsheet and the half-remembered mental maths that most households and small earners rely on. You record money in and money out, and the app keeps your accounts, budgets, bills, debts and savings goals organised in one calm dashboard — with no sign-up, no installation and no monthly fee.

On the dashboard you see your total balance across all accounts, this month's income, this month's expenses and your net position, together with a spending-by-category chart, an income-versus-expenses chart for the last six months, bills due in the next seven days and a debts-at-a-glance summary. Adding a transaction takes seconds: choose expense, income or transfer, enter the amount (a built-in calculator sits inside the amount field), pick a category and an account, and save. You can search, edit, delete and filter transactions by month, type, category and account whenever you need to check a detail.

Accounts, budgets, bills, debts and goals

The app starts with Cash, Bank, JazzCash and EasyPaisa accounts ready to use, and you can add your own custom accounts and move money between them with transfers. Budgets work on two levels: one overall monthly budget and separate budgets for each category, shown with progress bars that turn amber at 80 percent and red when a limit is passed, so overspending is visible before the month ends. Recurring rules handle rent, utility bills, subscriptions and salary on a weekly, monthly or yearly cycle, and due items appear on the dashboard ready to post with one tap. The debt tracker records money you lent and money you borrowed, including partial payments, so a friendly loan never becomes a forgotten argument. Savings goals let you name a target, set an amount and an optional date, and watch progress build.

Reports, export and your privacy

Monthly reports summarise where your money went, and you can print a monthly report directly from the app. Your data is yours to take with you: export all transactions as CSV, download a full backup as a JSON file, and restore that backup on any device. Nine currencies are supported — PKR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, KWD, INR and BDT — changing only the symbol and formatting, never converting amounts behind your back.

Privacy is the design, not a setting. Everything you enter is stored only in your own browser's local storage on your own device. There is no Loona server copy of your finances, no account to hack and nothing to leak. The honest trade-off is that clearing your browser data can remove your records, which is exactly why the JSON backup exists — download one regularly and keep it somewhere safe.

Who it helps, and who made it

Loona Finance Tracker is made for salaried workers tracking a monthly budget, freelancers with irregular income, students, shopkeepers, families sharing one household budget, and anyone who has ever asked where the money went. It works offline once loaded and can be installed as a PWA on your phone's home screen.

It is part of Loona Automation, founded by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer from Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan. His belief is simple: a useful tool should never put a door in front of the person who needs it — no account, no payment wall, no trial that expires mid-work. That is why this tracker is completely free, and why it will stay free.
