About Loona Finance Tracker

In short: Loona Finance Tracker is a free online finance tracker, expense tracker and budget planner that helps you record income and expenses, follow monthly budgets, manage recurring bills, track debts and grow savings goals. It needs no sign-up, works offline, and keeps all of your data on your own device.

Features

  • Dashboard at a glance — total balance, this month's income, expenses and net, a spending-by-category chart and an income-vs-expenses chart for the last 6 months.
  • Fast transactions — add income or an expense in seconds, with a built-in calculator in the amount field, plus edit, delete, search and filters by month, type, category and account.
  • Multiple accounts — Cash, Bank, JazzCash and EasyPaisa ready out of the box, plus your own custom accounts, with transfers between them.
  • Budgets that warn you — an overall monthly budget and per-category budgets with progress bars that turn amber at 80% and red when you pass the limit.
  • Bills & recurring items — weekly, monthly or yearly rules for rent, utility bills and salary; due items appear on your dashboard ready to post with one tap.
  • Debt tracker — money you lent or borrowed, with partial payments and remaining balances.
  • Savings goals — set a target, add contributions and watch the progress bar fill up.
  • Reports & export — monthly summaries, top expenses, CSV export, full JSON backup and restore, and a print-friendly monthly report.
  • Private by design — no account and no server copy of your data; everything lives in your browser on your device. Your old Loona Expense App entries are imported automatically.

How it works

1. Add your money. Tap − Expense, + Income or ⇄ Transfer, enter the amount (use the built-in calculator if you like), pick a category and account, and save. 2. Set your budgets. Add an overall monthly limit and per-category budgets so the app can warn you before you overspend. 3. Review and improve. Check the dashboard charts, post due bills with one tap, and export a monthly report to see exactly where your money went.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Loona Finance Tracker free?

Yes. Loona Finance Tracker is completely free — expense and income tracking, budgets, bills, debts and savings goals are all included, with no sign-up and no subscription.

Where is my financial data stored?

Only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to our servers, and there is no account, so your money data stays private. You can download a backup file any time from Reports.

Does the finance tracker work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the app works fully offline — add transactions, check budgets and view reports without any internet connection.

Can I export my transactions?

Yes. You can export all transactions as a CSV file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets, download a full JSON backup, and restore that backup later on any device.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. Open the page and start tracking immediately.

Which currencies does it support?

PKR (Pakistani Rupee) by default, plus USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, KWD, INR and BDT. The currency setting changes the symbol and formatting of your amounts.
