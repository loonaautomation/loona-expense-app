Finance Tracker questions we can help with

Export and backup: how to download a CSV of all transactions, how to save a full JSON backup, and how to restore that backup on a new phone or a different browser. If a restore does not look right, tell us which browser you exported from and which one you restored into.

Budgets, bills and recurring items: questions about overall and category budgets, progress bars, weekly, monthly or yearly recurring rules, and bills that did or did not appear on your dashboard when expected.

Accounts, debts and goals: transfers between accounts such as Cash, Bank, JazzCash and EasyPaisa, partial debt payments, savings goal dates, and currency display in PKR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, KWD, INR or BDT.

Reporting a bug

A useful bug report helps us fix things fast. Please include your device (for example an Android phone model or a Windows laptop), your browser and its version if you know it, and the exact steps that led to the problem — what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot is welcome. Please never email us your actual financial figures or a backup file containing real transactions; a description of the problem is enough, because your data belongs on your device, not in our inbox.

Feature ideas are equally welcome — many improvements to this tracker began as a user's email.
